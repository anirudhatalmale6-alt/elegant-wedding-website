Our Story

Twelve years, three cities, one very badly behaved dog.

Autumn 2015 · How we met

A queue for terrible coffee

We met in the worst café in Bristol, both queuing for a coffee neither of us enjoyed. Thomas claims he started the conversation. Eleanor has a witness who says otherwise. Twelve years later we still argue about it, usually over better coffee.

One rainy Tuesday became a Sunday walk, which became a standing arrangement, which became everything.

Summer 2019 · Making it home

A small flat and a large dog

We moved in together above a bakery — wonderful for six months, unbearable for the following two years. Then came Biscuit, who is a lurcher, entirely untrainable, and the reason we finally found a house with a garden.

He will not be at the celebration. He has been told. He is not taking it well.

New Year's Eve 2025 · The proposal

Eight minutes before midnight

On a cold beach in Northumberland, with a thermos of something warm and a speech that was abandoned halfway through, Thomas asked. Eleanor said yes before he had quite finished, which he maintains is cheating.

We spent the whole of January telling people, and the whole of February deciding that the wedding we actually wanted was a very quiet one — followed by a very loud party.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Can we come to the ceremony?

Honestly, no — and please don't read anything into it. The ceremony is the two of us and two witnesses in a register office, and there is no room for guests at all. We decided early on that we'd rather spend the day celebrating with everyone than managing a ceremony. The celebration from 2pm is the real event, and you're invited to all of it.

What time should I arrive?

Any time from 2pm. If you're coming on the coach it leaves The Swan at 1:15pm sharp. Dinner is served at 4pm, so please be seated by ten to.

Are children invited?

Yes, very much so. There's a children's menu on the RSVP form, and a quiet room with films and beanbags from 7pm for anyone who needs a break from the band.

Is there parking?

There is, and you're welcome to leave a car overnight and collect it before 11am the next morning. Taxis in the area need booking in advance — we'd suggest doing it now.

Can I bring a plus one?

If your invitation named a guest, absolutely — just add their name on the RSVP form. If it didn't, do get in touch and we'll see what we can do.

Who do I ask if something isn't answered here?

Our maid of honour Sarah is fielding everything on 07700 900 812, and would much rather you asked her than worried about it.
