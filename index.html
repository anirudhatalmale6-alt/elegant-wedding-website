Questions

Frequently Asked

Can we come to the ceremony?

It will be an intimate ceremony with just us, the boys and our immediate family members, held earlier in the day. We can't wait to share the memories with you, and then celebrate into the evening together. The reception is the real event and you're invited to every minute of it.

What time should I arrive?

Please be at The Love House by 4:30pm, ready for a 5:00pm start. That way we get the formalities done nice and early and can get straight into the fun part of the night.

Can I bring a guest?

The general rule is that if you're married or engaged, your partner is invited. If you're unsure, or we've specified something different on your invitation, please check with us.

Can I bring my kids?

We want to make sure you can truly relax and enjoy a night off celebrating with us, so we kindly ask that you organise a sitter for your children for the evening. Thank you so much for understanding.

Where can I stay?

We recommend booking accommodation early, as Anglesea can get busy in the summer months. There are plenty of beautiful places to stay in Anglesea, with nearby towns like Torquay and Aireys Inlet also offering great options.

Does the venue have parking?

There is parking available at the venue, however spaces are limited. If the main car park is full, there is street parking available within a short walking distance of The Love House.

Will there be any transport from the venue?

Yes. A bus will be provided back to Torquay after the event, with drop-off at the Torquay Hotel. Ubers can be very difficult to get in Anglesea, so please tell us on the RSVP form whether you'll use it, and we'll share the final details closer to the date.

What exactly is the dress code?

Dress code is semi-formal. Boys, think a white shirt paired with comfortable shorts or pants, and something you can relax and celebrate in. Girls, think cute summer dress or something comfortable but still a little dressed up. You know Dani's not big on a full cocktail moment, so no need to go over the top, just something you feel great in.

Who do I ask about anything else?

Just message Danielle or Jordan directly. You should already have our contact details.
